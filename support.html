Common Questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send you a secure link to set a new password. The link expires after one hour.

I want to delete my account

Sign in, open Settings, and choose Delete Account. Your account, posts, comments, messages, and uploaded media are permanently removed. If you cannot sign in, email hello@blipvibe.com from the email address on the account and we will process the deletion for you.

I want to delete some of my data (without deleting my account)

You can delete any of your own BlipVibe content at any time from within the app: open the menu (⋯) on a post or comment and choose Delete, or delete individual messages from a conversation. Deleted content is removed from our active database immediately; residual copies may persist in automated backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten. To request deletion of other specific data (for example, your profile photo history or stories), email hello@blipvibe.com from the email address on your account and tell us what you'd like removed — we will process the request within 30 days. Account information required to operate the service (such as your email address and username) is kept until you delete your account.

I'm having trouble uploading a video or photo

Make sure your file is under 500 MB and that your network connection is stable. iPhone HEVC clips are supported. If the upload still fails, please email us with the device, browser, and a description of what you saw.

How do I report a user, post, or message?

Open the menu (⋯) on the post, comment, or profile in question and choose Report. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.

How do I block someone?

Visit their profile and choose Block. Blocked users cannot see your content, message you, or follow you.

I want a copy of my data

Email hello@blipvibe.com from the email on your account and request an export. We will send you a copy of your account data within 30 days.

Safety & Trust

Reporting abuse, hate speech, or illegal content

BlipVibe does not tolerate hate speech, harassment, or illegal content. If you encounter material that violates our Community Guidelines or applicable law, please use the in-app Report option or email us directly. Urgent safety concerns may be sent to hello@blipvibe.com with the subject line "URGENT".

Copyright (DMCA) takedowns

To submit a DMCA takedown notice, please see our DMCA policy.

Children & minors

BlipVibe is not directed at children under 13. If you believe an account belongs to a child under 13, please email hello@blipvibe.com and we will investigate and remove the account if confirmed.
