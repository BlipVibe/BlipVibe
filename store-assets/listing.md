## Full description (4000 chars max)
BlipVibe is a fresh, friendly place to share your world and connect with your people.

POST YOUR WAY
Share text, photos, videos, and GIFs. Add music to your profile so visitors get your vibe the moment they arrive.

STORIES
Post stories that disappear after 24 hours. See who viewed them, and comment on your friends' stories.

REAL CONVERSATIONS
Comment, reply, react with emoji, and chat directly with friends or in group conversations. Pinch to zoom on any photo.

EARN AS YOU GO
Collect coins for posting, commenting, and daily logins. Spend them on profile skins and customizations that make your page yours.

COMMUNITIES
Join groups around what you love, meet new people, and grow your circle.

MAKE IT YOURS
Dark mode, light mode, custom profile skins, cover photos, and profile music.

BlipVibe is free. Your content belongs to you — read our privacy policy at https://blipvibe.com/privacy.html
