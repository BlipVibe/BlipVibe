## Owner direction (Nov 16)
- Profile = the concept profile (sheet 1): huge moon top centre, two tall gothic side arches + a wide scalloped bottom arch
  holding the 3 cover photos, round rose cameo in the middle, candelabras either side of the name. Painted separately as
  `profile-sky` + `profile-windows` (`prototypes/blood-moon-profile-brief.md`).
- Someone else's profile uses the SAME art as Home ("I want the same artwork as the home feed"), not sheet 2's three arches
  (`gothic-windows` is now unused).
- No holes and no spill around the cover photos: each photo is masked by its own `profile-mask-1..3` (left / bottom / right),
  generated by `prototypes/tools/blood_moon_profile_masks.py`: every transparent area inside the frame's silhouette, split by
  nearest window, grown only under the opaque frame. Slot boxes in the CSS come from its printout.
- Long names wrap by whole words beside the flourishes (tested "Alexandria Montgomery"); added to the Skin-QA list, along with
  an unreadable-text check.
- Top-bar icons sit in dark round silver buttons (concept) so they read on Silver's bright sky.

- Gotcha: the parchment-card ink rule uses `:is(.card:not(..):not(..))`, which outweighs plain selectors: dark-velvet pieces
  inside cards (the composer) need an id-anchored rule.

## Animations (own)
- Equip (`equipBloodMoon`): MOONRISE: night falls, the spires rise, the giant moon (of the rendition you'll see) climbs and
  swells to fill the screen, then bursts into a whirl of rose petals (red / white) that blow away.
- Crimson ↔ Silver (`bloodSwap`, the moon button in the Home top bar): an ECLIPSE: the earth's shadow slides across the
  giant moon, and as it passes it leaves the other moon behind; the sky turns, petals fall, the sky clears.
  Smooth pass (owner: "isn't very smooth"): one continuous rAF timeline (no phase joins), the other rendition's art
  fetched + decoded first, skies pre-rendered, the skin switches at totality under the dark moon, holds softly until the
  new art is ready, then the sky fades out. Measured in the harness: no frozen frames, 2 single-frame hitches.
