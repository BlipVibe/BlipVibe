# Witchcraft — production art traced from the approved concept

Owner: build the skin's assets FROM THE CONCEPT. Every piece below must look like its counterpart in the
concept crops — same shape, proportions, ornament, material and colour. Do not invent a different style.

Material (copy the concept): painted dark-fantasy realism — carved dark wood (near-black brown), aged
bronze / antique gold trim with small fleur and pointed-gothic ornaments, deep violet/magenta glow, warm
candlelight, faint purple haze. Readable and calm in the middle of panels (text sits on them).

References (one crop per phone screen, 3× upscaled) in `~/Developer/BlipVibe/prototypes/reference/witchcraft/`:
home, skins, profile, groups, post, messages, chat (sheet 1) and home-full, network, notifications, search,
group-page, other-profile, comments, create-post, story (sheet 2). Full sheets:
`images/skins/witchcraft/concept-v3-approved.png` (sheet 1) and `images/skins/witchcraft/concept-2.png` (sheet 2).

Rules: NO text, NO letters, NO avatars/photos/people (the app supplies those). Real alpha (transparent PNG)
outside every shape. Frames have fully transparent windows. Every piece is drawn WHOLE at the stated size (the
app stretches it to its element) — so for panels/rows/buttons keep ornaments near the corners and keep the long
edges plain enough to stretch. State nothing in the image except the art.
