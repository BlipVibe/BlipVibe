# Scrapbook — DESKTOP kit part 3: the top bar (repaint)
The first `topbar-wide.png` came out as a thin plain pink line. The concept's top bar (look at the top ~75px of
`images/skins/scrapbook/desktop/desktop-home.png`) is a RICH, full-height band: layered torn pink and cream papers over
a strip of old map/ledger paper, fibrous torn bottom edge with a little lace peeking under it, washi tape pieces and a
paperclip at the far left, small pressed flowers at both ends. Realistic photographed paper, soft shadow under it.
Save `images/skins/scrapbook/kit/desktop/topbar-wide.png` — 2560x130, real alpha below the torn bottom edge only
(the band itself is opaque and fills the full width and roughly the top 110px). It is stretched/tiled horizontally on
screens 1100–2560 wide, so the middle ~1900px must be a consistent busy paper texture with no single object that would
look smeared. Paint on #00FF00 below the edge if needed and key it out with Pillow.
