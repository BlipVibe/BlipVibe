# #Basic v3 — "basic girl" redesign around the nine-square profile (owner, Nov 19 2026)

BlipVibe is a social app. Its "full skins" repaint every screen with generated art. #Basic is the "basic girl" skin.
The owner loves the new NINE-SQUARE profile (3x3 photo grid, profile picture in the middle) and wants the WHOLE skin
redesigned around it. Paint CONCEPT SHEETS of every page listed below — only the pages and fields listed here (they
are exactly what the real app has; do not invent extra features, do not drop any).

## The vibe — "basic girl", done beautifully
The cosy, unapologetically basic girl: iced oat-milk latte with a reusable straw, an oversized beige tumbler
(unbranded), claw clip, gold hoops, vanilla candle, ribbed knit, cream sneakers, pilates socks, a "that girl" planner,
little satin BOWS. Soft, warm, pretty, a bit coquette — never loud.
- Palette: cream, vanilla, oat, latte, warm sand, espresso ink. Accent per rendition (below).
- Shapes: soft rounded cards (corner radius ~22pt) of warm cream with a faint ribbed-knit / linen texture, a thin
  lighter top edge, a soft warm shadow. Pills for chips, buttons and fields. Tight, tidy spacing (8–10pt gutters) — the
  owner hates "too spaced out".
- Tiny satin bow accents (one per screen at most: the profile ring, the active dock tab, a section title), never more.
- Type: all lowercase. Rounded friendly sans for UI; a soft italic serif for one accent word per title
  ("daily *routine*", "hey *girl*"). Espresso text on cream, always readable, 16pt minimum for anything you type into.
- Icons: chunky rounded line icons in espresso; ON states (liked, disliked, active tab) are FILLED in the accent.

### Two renditions (switched by the season button in the top bar) — paint each one independently
- **ROSÉ** (spring/summer): cream + dusty blush pink (#c98f86) + rose-gold hairlines; blush satin bows; photos of iced
  lattes, peonies, beach days, white linen, matcha, pilates. Season button = an iced latte in a clear cup.
- **PUMPKIN SPICE** (autumn): cream + caramel / muted pumpkin (#b8754a) + gold hairlines; caramel velvet bows;
  photos of a pumpkin spice latte, chunky knit, a ceramic pumpkin, autumn leaves, a vanilla candle. Season button = a
  ribbed mug with steam.

## Must look nothing like the other BlipVibe skins
No torn paper, tape or polaroids (Scrapbook); no comic panels (Anime Blaze / Superhero); no gothic arches or potions
(Witchcraft / Blood Moon); no clouds or stars (Good Witch); no 90s window bevels (Vaporwave); no marble, columns,
laurels or ALL-CAPS Roman serif (Heaven's Light).

## On EVERY page
- **Top bar** (frosted cream, under the status bar): small round profile photo, "#blipvibe" wordmark, then round icon
  buttons: search, bell (accent count badge), music note, a coin pill ("2,450" — or "∞"), the Barista Bar button (a
  tiny coffee cup on a saucer) and the season button. Sub-pages show a back chevron + the page title instead of the
  wordmark, the buttons stay.
- **Dock** (bottom nav, floating rounded frosted bar): 5 tabs — home "#", groups, skins, network, messages; the active
  tab is an accent tile with a tiny bow and its lowercase label; the others are cream tiles.

## THE NINE-SQUARE PROFILE WIDGET (Home and both Profile pages) — the heart of the skin
One big cream card, full width. Inside it, a 3x3 grid of photo squares (slightly wider than tall, ~117:103, 4pt
gutters, rounded 12pt corners, thin cream outline): the cover photo top-middle, 7 more of the user's photos around.
**The MIDDLE square is the profile picture and it is a LITTLE BIGGER than the other squares** — a round photo in a
thick cream ring with a satin bow at the top of the ring, about 15% bigger than a square, so it slightly overlaps the
gutters onto the neighbouring photos and sits on top of them (a little camera badge on the ring on your own profile).
Under the grid, centred: name with an italic heart ("rion ♡"), a one-line bio in italic serif, two stat pills
("35 following" · "21 followers"), and:
- on Home: a "view profile ✎" pill;
- on your own Profile page: a full-width accent "edit profile" button;
- on someone else's Profile: a row "follow" (accent) · "message" (cream) · "⋯" (round cream). Show one sheet with the
  ⋯ menu open: a small frosted menu card with mute · close friend · invite to group · block · report (block and
  report in a soft red).
