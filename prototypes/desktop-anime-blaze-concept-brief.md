Look: clean, bold anime/manga comic ILLUSTRATION (not realistic): dark ink page, halftone dots, speed lines, slashes,
flames at the edges, white-ink sketchy borders, red slanted active tiles, zigzag cuts, Barlow Semi Condensed bold italic.
Desktop STRUCTURE (unique to this skin): a MANGA PAGE built from DIAGONAL panels. Navigation = a tall slanted
red-and-black VERTICAL RAIL down the LEFT edge (wordmark rotated at the top, icon tabs stacked, the active tab a red
slanted tile, coins/music/avatar at the bottom of the rail), search as a slashed field at the top of the page. Home:
a huge full-width SPLASH PANEL cut by diagonal zigzag gutters across the top (one cover photo, the round photo on the
cut, the name in a red slanted name tag with stats), then the feed as staggered angled panels in TWO uneven columns
(posts wide, quests/coins/people as smaller tilted panels slotted between them), speed lines and flames in every
gutter. Messages = chat as a big panel with the conversation list as a stack of slanted strips. Groups/Skins =
angled panel grids of different sizes (not an even grid). Cover photos: 1.
