# #Basic v2 — "nine squares" profile concept (owner, Nov 17 2026)

The owner wants the profile area on Home (and on the Profile page) remade as **9 squares in a 3x3 grid with the
profile picture in the middle square**. Everything else stays the approved #Basic "aesthetic widgets" look: read
`prototypes/basic-widgets-concept-brief.md` and look at the approved Home crop `prototypes/reference/basic-w-<r>/home.png`
and the sheet `images/skins/basic/concept-w-<r>.png` — same wallpaper, frosted cream glass widgets, beige icon tiles,
top bar, floating dock, lowercase rounded type, palette.

## The nine-square profile widget (top of Home, full width under the top bar)
- One large frosted-glass widget holding a tidy **3x3 grid of equal SQUARE tiles** with tight even gutters (~6pt),
  iOS-widget squircle corners on each tile, the whole grid square and full width.
- **Middle square = the profile picture**: the round photo of the person inside the thick cream glass ring, sitting on
  a cream glass tile (or filling the tile, rounded) — clearly the hero of the grid. A tiny camera tile badge on its
  corner (edit photo).
- **The 8 squares around it = the person's timeline photos** (edge-to-edge photos, rounded): realistic placeholder
  photos (Rosé: iced latte, peonies, beach, white linen bed, brunch, sunset, travertine bathroom, a friend selfie;
  Pumpkin: knit throw, ceramic pumpkin, hot latte, autumn leaves, candle, forest walk, apple pie, cozy reading nook).
  The top-middle square is the cover photo. A tiny white date label ("jun 14") in a corner of two or three of them.
- Under the grid, inside the same widget, centred: name in lowercase with an italic accent + a small heart, a short
  italic bio line, two stat chips "412 following" · "1,286 followers", and a small glass pill "view profile ✎".
- Below that widget, the rest of Home continues as approved: coin widget + featured skin widget side by side, the
  "daily routine" widget, tabs, posts, then the floating dock with "home" lit.
