# BlipVibe full-skin system — decisions so far

## Process (per skin, one at a time)
1. Codex/ChatGPT makes a 7-screen **master concept** (Home, Skins, Profile, Groups, Post view,
   Messages, Chat) saved to `images/skins/<id>/concept.png`. Owner approves before anything is built.
2. Codex generates that skin's production assets from the approved concept into `images/skins/<id>/`.
3. The skin is built into the engine.

## When sheet 1 and sheet 2 disagree
Sheet 1's layout choices win (e.g. Kawaii Cats keeps Daily Quests BELOW the feed tabs, even though its
sheet 2 Home shows them above). Sheet 2 is the reference for the extra screens only.

## Shipping (no App Store / Play resubmit needed)
- Skins are web-layer only (JS/CSS/HTML + images) → reach iOS/Android via the existing OTA bundle
  (`.github/workflows/publish-ota.yml`, runs on push to main) and the site via GitHub Pages.
- OTA bundles everything under `images/`, so before merging:
  - move concept sheets/backups out of `images/` into a non-shipping design folder (e.g. `design/skins/`)
  - convert skin assets to compressed WebP
  - load each skin's art on demand (when equipped/previewed) instead of bundling every skin into the OTA payload

## Fidelity — the build must match the approved concepts almost 1:1
- The approved concept sheets are the visual spec: same shapes, materials, icons, colors, positions, decorations.
- For every element in a concept:
  - **Real feature** → build it to look exactly like the concept.
  - **Wrong text/data** → keep the look, swap in the real field / live data.
  - **Invented feature the app doesn't have** (generator made it up) → do not fake it; omit and flag to owner.
- Production assets are generated by Codex **from that skin's concept.png on disk** so they match it.
- Acceptance per skin: side-by-side screenshots (real app vs concept) for every screen, iterate until
  they match, then owner signs off.

## Screen coverage — every skin must theme ALL of these
- **Concept sheet 1 (key screens):** Home feed, Skins shop, own Profile, Groups list, Post view, Messages list, Chat.
- **Concept sheet 2 (rest of the app):** Network (people), Notifications, Search, Saved, Photos,
  Group page (group-view), Other user's profile (profile-view), My Skins tab, Comments / open post,
  Create post, Story viewer + story creation, Photo lightbox, Quest panel, Top menu dropdown,
  Settings/Privacy, toasts and tutorial overlay.
- **Engine guarantee:** every surface uses the skin's panels, buttons, tabs, icons, fonts and
  background, so nothing falls back to the old look even where no concept was drawn.
- **Left on the standard look:** admin panel, login/sign-up, legal pages (internal or pre-login).

## Design rules for every skin
- **Shared:** only the screens and the real app fields/labels (see `scrapbook-field-corrections.md`).
- **Unique per skin:** materials, card/frame shapes, decorations, nav icons and nav style, fonts,
  quest presentation, and how the profile timeline photos are shown.
- **Backgrounds stay calm and low-contrast**, with detail at the edges, so content is always readable.
- Signature flourishes belong to one skin only (e.g. polaroids/washi tape = Scrapbook only).
- **Layouts differ too, not just decoration.** Rotate placements so no two skins share an arrangement:
  - Profile picture: centered under cover · left, overlapping the cover's lower-left corner with name/bio
    to its right · top-right badge · inside the cover frame · large hero with cover as a band behind ·
    beside a vertical cover strip
  - Header: logo left · logo centered (coins left) · logo stacked over icons · icons in a floating pill
  - Daily quests: above feed tabs · below feed tabs · horizontal row · collapsible strip · side rail
  - Bottom nav: bar · floating dock · arc/curved · tab "shelf" · pill with raised centre button
  - Stats/buttons: row · badges · stacked list
  - Profile picture SHAPE varies too (applied in code as a mask over the real photo):
    Scrapbook circle · Witchcraft tarot card · Good Witch soft five-point star ·
    Anime Blaze slanted parallelogram with burst edge · Kawaii Cats cat paw print ·
    reserved for upcoming skins: heart, square, hexagon, gem, shield, etc.
  - Assigned profile layouts:
    - Scrapbook — avatar centered under the two polaroids (owner's master; keep)
    - Witchcraft — avatar IS the centre card of the tarot spread; name/bio on a carved plaque below
    - Good Witch — avatar inside a crescent moon to the side of the moon phases; name/stats stacked beside it
    - Anime Blaze — avatar as a top-right character cut-in on the manga cover; name/stats in slanted callouts
    - Kawaii Cats — avatar left, overlapping the cat-head cover's lower-left corner; name/bio to its right;
      logo centered in header; quests as a bowl row below the feed tabs

## Profile timeline photos
The frames behind the profile picture show the **user's real photos** with a skin-specific filter
applied in code (CSS filters, tint overlays, frame art), so any photo looks native to the skin.
Count and frame vary per skin:

| Skin | Count | Frame | Photo filter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scrapbook | 2 | polaroids | warm faded film |
| Witchcraft | 4 | fanned tarot cards, engraved gold borders | moonlit purple tint, vignette, faint stars |
| Good Witch | 3 | moon-phase circles (waxing/full/waning) | soft pastel glow / bloom |
| Anime Blaze | 1 | slanted manga cover panel | red-orange duotone, halftone, ink outline |

Source of photos: slot 1 = `cover_photo_url_mobile` (or `cover_photo_url`); further slots = the
user's latest photos from the Photos tab; fall back to alternate crops of the cover photo.

## Other shop items while a full skin is equipped (decided)
- **Skin decides (turned off while a full skin is on):** icon sets, nav styles, layout templates, logo styles.
- **Still apply on top:** fonts (optional override, default = the skin's own font), coin skins, songs.
- Nothing is taken away: every purchase stays owned and works again on any basic skin.

## Asset kit (per skin)
Standard brief: `prototypes/asset-kit-brief.md`. Each skin gets `images/skins/<id>/kit/` with
background, icons (20, sprite), panels, buttons, nav strips, frames (+ avatar mask), quest states,
six decorations, and a thumbnail. Sprite sheets are 2048² transparent grids that get sliced in code.
One Codex chat per skin; ~5–6 in parallel, watching throttling and the Usage meter.
