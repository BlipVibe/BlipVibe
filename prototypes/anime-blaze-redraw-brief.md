# Anime Blaze REDRAW — every UI piece traced from the concept, in its hand-drawn comic style

Owner verdict on sets 1–4: "too clean". The concept has a **very drawn, sketchy comic-book look**: rough
black brush/ink outlines with varying thickness, dry-brush scratches and nicks, screentone halftone dots,
speed/hatch lines, slightly irregular corners, white strokes that are themselves outlined in black ink,
red→orange flame brush slashes and embers. Our pieces look like clean vectors — REDRAW them to match.

References (study closely; one crop per phone screen, 3× upscaled):
`~/Developer/BlipVibe/prototypes/reference/anime-blaze/*.png` — home, skins, profile, groups, post,
messages, chat (sheet 1) and home-full, network, notifications, search, group-page, other-profile,
comments, create-post, story (sheet 2). Full sheets: `images/skins/anime-blaze/concept-v1-approved.png`,
`concept-2.png`. **Copy each piece from its crop — same shape, slant, outline weight, flames, texture.**

Rules: no text, no icons/glyphs, no avatars/photos (the app supplies those). Real alpha outside shapes.
Frames have fully transparent windows. Every piece is drawn whole at the stated size (the app stretches
it to the element), so draw the complete shape. Colour = the concept's red/orange (the app recolours later).
