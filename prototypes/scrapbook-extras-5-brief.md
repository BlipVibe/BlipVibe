Target: prototypes/reference/concept-post.png (and the posts in scrapbook-master-v2.png).

foxing) → a torn coloured paper scrap across the TOP of the backing → washi tape on the backing's
corner → the soft cream post card on top → tape over the photo's corner → a lighter paper strip
behind the action row. Posts will mix these pieces so each looks a little different.
