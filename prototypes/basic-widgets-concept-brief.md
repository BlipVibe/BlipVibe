# #Basic v2 — "aesthetic widgets" concept sheets (redesign, owner Nov 17 2026)

The first #Basic (a painted oak shelf with framed prints) did not look good in the real app. The owner picked a full
redesign: **the whole app looks like a beige "aesthetic iPhone home screen"** — the clean-girl phone setup people post:
neutral wallpaper, rounded WIDGETS in a bento grid, a matching beige icon pack, a frosted dock. Keep the clean-girl
neutral palette and the two seasonal renditions (Rosé, Pumpkin Spice) and the Barista Bar. Throw away the shelf, the
oak frames, the place card, the oak tray nav — none of that appears any more.

BlipVibe is a social app. Its "full skins" repaint every screen with generated art. Paint the CONCEPT SHEETS.

## The look — aesthetic widgets
- **Wallpaper (every page):** a soft, slightly blurred neutral photo wallpaper behind the whole app — sunlight and a
  linen-curtain shadow falling across a cream / oat limewash wall (Rosé: a hint of blush light; Pumpkin: golden
  afternoon light). Calm, low-contrast, so the widgets read on top of it.
- **Widgets = every card.** Every panel, row group, post, quest list, list, chat thread and menu is a WIDGET: a soft
  squircle tile (iOS-widget corner radius, ~22pt) of frosted cream glass with a very fine linen/bouclé texture inside,
  a hairline lighter edge on top, a soft realistic drop shadow. Widgets sit in a tidy BENTO GRID with even, TIGHT
  gutters (about 10–12pt) — compact and full like a real home screen, NOT airy, no big empty gaps.
  Sizes follow the widget grid: small (square), medium (wide, 2:1), large (square, 2x2 of small).
- **Icon pack:** chunky, rounded, friendly line glyphs (heavy ~2.5px-at-24pt stroke, round caps) in deep espresso
  brown, each sitting on its own little beige squircle "app icon" tile (cream → oat gradient, soft inner shading).
  This tile-icon look is used for the nav, the top-bar buttons, list leading icons and menu icons.
- **Type:** everything lowercase. Rounded geometric sans (like a skincare / iced-coffee brand) for titles and body,
  small italic serif accents for a word here and there ("hey, *you*"). Big friendly widget numbers (like a clock or
  step-count widget) for stats, coin balance and quest progress. Espresso ink on cream, always readable.
- **Accents:** Rosé = dusty blush with a rose-gold hairline; Pumpkin Spice = caramel / muted pumpkin with a gold
  hairline. Used for the active dock icon, primary buttons, progress fills, liked/disliked ON icons (FILLED solid),
  toggles and badges. Never hot pink, never bright orange, no neon.

## MUST LOOK COMPLETELY DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER BLIPVIBE SKINS (owner rule)
Scrapbook (torn paper collage, tape, polaroids, handwriting), Anime Blaze (black/red comic panels), Witchcraft (dark
purple gothic arches, potions), Good Witch (lilac clouds, constellations), Vaporwave (90s OS windows, grey bevels,
Start taskbar), Heaven's Light (ivory marble, ornate gold laurels/wings, columns, arches, ALL-CAPS Roman serif), Blood
Moon (gothic red/silver filigree), Superhero (comic city). So: no paper, no tape, no polaroids, no bevels, no arches,
no columns, no ornament, no all-caps, no Roman serif, no window title bars. #Basic is MODERN, SOFT, GLASSY, TIDY.

## Layout of each screen
- **Top bar (every page):** a frosted cream glass bar under the status bar: small round profile photo, "#blipvibe"
  wordmark (lowercase rounded sans), then icon-tile buttons: search, bell (blush/caramel count badge), music note, a
  coin pill "2,450 +", the Barista Bar button (a tiny coffee cup on a saucer) and the season button (Rosé: a clear iced
  latte glass; Pumpkin: a ribbed mug with steam). Sub-pages show a back chevron tile + lowercase title instead.
- **Home (the bento):**
  1. PROFILE WIDGET (large, full width, ~1.4:1): the cover photo fills the top of the widget edge to edge (rounded top
     corners); the round profile picture (thick cream glass ring + soft shadow) overlaps the cover's bottom-left edge;
     right of it: name in lowercase with an italic accent + a small heart, bio line, then two stat chips
     "412 following" · "1,286 followers". This widget MUST always show on Home.
  2. PHOTO WIDGETS row: the 3 timeline photos as three small square photo widgets side by side (rounded, edge-to-edge
     photo, tiny white date/caption label in the corner like a "memories" widget).
  3. Two small widgets side by side: COIN widget (big number "2,450", a ring progress toward the coin goal, "coin
     goal 5,000") and FEATURED SKIN widget (skin thumbnail + "featured" + name + "300" chip).
  4. DAILY QUESTS = a "reminders / to-do" widget (medium-tall): title "daily routine" with an italic accent, 3 rows
     with round tick circles, the text (react to 5 posts · send 3 messages · like 5 posts), a slim progress bar with
     3/5, 1/3, 4/5, a tiny latte-glass progress icon that fills up (empty → half → full) and a little reward chip
     (+5, +10, +5). One row ticked.
  5. TABS as a segmented control widget: following / discover / my posts (active segment = accent pill).
  6. POSTS: each post is a large widget: author row (round avatar, name, "2h ago", ⋯), lowercase caption, the photo
     inset with rounded corners, then an action row of chunky icons: like (one post LIKED = filled accent heart),
     dislike (one post DISLIKED = filled), comment, share with counts.
- **Dock (bottom nav):** a floating frosted glass DOCK (like the iPhone dock, rounded, inset from the screen edges)
  holding 5 beige app-icon tiles: home "#", groups, skins, network, messages. The active one is the accent-coloured
  tile (blush / caramel) with a tiny lowercase label under it; the others are beige tiles.
- **Profile:** same profile widget large at top + an "edit profile" accent pill button, the 3 photo widgets, a
  segmented control posts / media / liked / saved, then a grid of photo widgets.
- **Skins:** title row "skins — *change your vibe*", filter chips (all / aesthetic / realistic / scrapbook / animated)
  as small glass pills, then a 2-column grid of skin widgets (preview art fills the top, name + tagline, price chip
  "300" with a coin, or "equipped ✓" on #Basic).
- **Groups / Messages / Notifications / Network / Search:** title row with an icon tile, a pill search field (16px
  text, magnifier icon), segmented chips, then LIST WIDGETS: rows grouped inside one widget with hairline dividers
  (round avatar or square photo, name, sub-line, time, accent unread badge).
- **Chat:** contact header (back tile, avatar, name, "active now", call/video tiles); received bubbles = cream frosted
  glass, sent bubbles = accent (blush / caramel) glass, a photo bubble; input = frosted pill with a "+" tile and a round
  accent send button.
- **Post view / Comments / Create post / Story / Group page / someone else's Profile (⋯ menu open: mute / block /
  report as a small frosted menu widget):** all built from the same widgets.

## Two renditions — switched with the season button in the top bar
Same widget world, each painted independently (never a recolour/filter of the other).
- **ROSÉ (spring/summer):** cream, oat, sand + dusty blush; wallpaper with soft blush morning light; photos of iced
  lattes, peonies, beach, white linen. Season button = clear iced latte.
- **PUMPKIN SPICE (autumn):** cream, oat, camel, toffee + muted pumpkin/caramel; wallpaper with golden late light and a
  dried-leaf shadow; photos of knit throws, a ceramic pumpkin, a hot latte, autumn leaves. Season button = ribbed mug.

## Signature interaction — the BARISTA BAR (show it)
The cup button opens the Barista Bar: a menu WIDGET drops from the top bar — frosted cream glass (Rosé: blush hairline;
Pumpkin: caramel/kraft tint, gold hairline), titled "barista bar" with a cup emblem, items written like a café menu with
dotted leaders and small tile icons on the right: my profile · saved posts · skins · settings · switch to pumpkin spice
(in Rosé) / switch to rosé (in Pumpkin) · latte break · last call.
- Latte Break: a full-screen top-down latte in a ribbed cup on a travertine saucer, milk pouring latte art, steam,
  "tap to sip".
- Last Call: a takeaway cup with the user's name scribbled MISSPELLED ("Daynee ♡"), a linen awning lowering with
  "closed — see you tomorrow".
