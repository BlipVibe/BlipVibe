# Good Witch — production art traced from the approved concept (complete, one pass)

Owner: build the skin's assets FROM THE CONCEPT and match it ALMOST 1:1. Every piece must look like its counterpart in the concept crops —
same shape, proportion, ornament, material, colour. Do not invent a different style. (Lesson from Witchcraft: the
owner wants EVERYTHING visible in the concept — icons, badges, frames, props — so this list is the full inventory.)

Material (copy the concept): soft pastel fairy-tale magic — lavender / lilac / blush-pink, pearly frosted GLASS panels
(milky white-lilac, slightly translucent, soft inner glow), thin warm GOLD filigree edges with tiny sparkles, gold
crescent moons and stars, dreamy cloud sky. Clean, bright, airy, crisp edges (no muddy blur). Text sits on the glass,
so panel centres stay calm and light.

References (3x crops, one per phone screen): `~/Developer/BlipVibe/prototypes/reference/good-witch/`
home, skins, profile, groups, post, messages, chat (sheet 1) and home-full, network, notifications, search, group-page,
other-profile, comments, create-post, story (sheet 2). Full sheets: `images/skins/good-witch/concept-v1-approved.png`,
`images/skins/good-witch/concept-2.png`.

Rules: NO text, NO letters, NO avatars/photos/people (the app supplies those). Transparent PNG (real alpha) outside every
shape; frames have fully transparent windows. Draw each piece WHOLE at the stated size; for panels/rows/buttons keep
ornament in the corners and long edges plain so they stretch. Small pieces (icons, badges, moons) must be razor sharp.
Save into `~/Developer/BlipVibe/images/skins/good-witch/kit/gw/` with the exact file names. Finish with a contact sheet,
each piece beside its crop.
