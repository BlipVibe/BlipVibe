# Walnut Hi-Fi (Blip Vibe `lofi-room`) — production art kit

A look of the default skin `blip` (variant `lofi-room`, js/fullskins.js FULL_SKINS.blip.variants).
Style truth: `images/skins/default/lofi-room/{home,group,desktop}.png` + the per-page concepts in the same folder (local only).
Mascot: `images/skins/blip/ref/logo-original.png`. Logo Classic's kit (`images/skins/blip/kit/*.png`, brief
`prototypes/blip-kit-brief.md`) is the PART LIST: same file names, same canvas sizes, same purpose — painted in Walnut Hi-Fi.

## Look (copy the concepts)
Cozy record room / listening session. Warm walnut wood `#6B4226` (grain visible, satin finish) for bars, frames and
cabinet edges; cream `#F6EBD9` card faces with a thin walnut-brown edge and a soft warm shadow; burnt orange `#E8742A`
for primary/active (glossy-ish enamel pill with a faint top highlight); olive `#7A8B4A` secondary pills; woven
speaker-grille / burlap cloth for heading strips (Photos, Daily Quests, Members, Group Posts); brushed aluminium for
knobs, round push-buttons and small screw heads in card corners; analogue VU meters; vinyl records. Text colour dark
brown `#3B2414`. The mascot's blue appears only as glowing indicator lights (active nav dot).
NOT dark navy, NOT purple, NOT neon, NOT glass, NOT oak-shelf beige café (#Basic), NO gold/marble.

## Rules
- NO text, letters or numbers in any piece. NO photos or people.
- REAL transparent alpha outside every shape. NEVER paint a grey/white checkerboard.
- Frames have fully transparent windows. 9-slice pieces: corners hold the shape, long edges plain and uniform.
- Draw each piece WHOLE at its canvas size, centred, nothing cut off. Icons razor sharp, one family.
- Liked/disliked ON states clearly FILLED (burnt orange) so the change is obvious.
- Mascot = the ORIGINAL logo character, unchanged. NO ARMS, NO HANDS, NO BODY, NO LEGS.

## Icons (256x256, transparent): rounded line icons, dark walnut-brown `#3B2414` stroke ~14px, one family
Same names as Logo Classic's kit. `*-on` nav icons = same icon in cream `#F6EBD9` (on the orange active button).
icon-like-on / icon-dislike-on FILLED burnt orange with a dark brown outline. icon-exit + icon-flag-red drawn in red `#C0392B`.
quest-* (200x200) = white line icon on a burnt-orange rounded square tile with a darker orange edge (concept Daily Quests).
notif-* (200x200) = cream line icon in a burnt-orange disc with a thin walnut ring.
