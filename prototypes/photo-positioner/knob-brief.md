# Photo positioner — zoom slider knob for each full skin (Nov 16)

The new photo positioner (zoom + move a photo inside the skin's frame) has a zoom slider. Its track and fill reuse each
skin's existing bar art; the only new piece is the slider KNOB (thumb) the user drags. Paint one knob per skin, matching
that skin's existing art exactly (open the files listed to match material, palette, line weight and lighting).

Every knob: square canvas 256x256, REAL transparent background (alpha, no checkerboard, no backdrop, no shadow box),
the knob centred and filling about 80% of the canvas, seen straight on, no text, no letters. It must read clearly
on both light and dark backgrounds (give it its own outline/rim).
