Child Safety Standards

BlipVibe's published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Last updated: July 16, 2026.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

BlipVibe LLC ("BlipVibe") strictly prohibits any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors. This includes, without limitation: child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, the solicitation of minors, sexualized commentary about minors, and the trafficking or facilitation of any of the above. Violations result in immediate content removal, permanent account termination, and reporting to the appropriate authorities.

Who may use BlipVibe

BlipVibe requires all users to be at least 13 years old, with parental or guardian consent required for users under 18, as set out in our Terms of Service. Accounts found to belong to children under 13 are removed.

In-app reporting

Every post, comment, message, and profile on BlipVibe can be reported directly in the app: open the menu (⋯) on the content or profile and choose Report. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team, with child safety reports treated at the highest priority. Users can also report concerns by email at hello@blipvibe.com.

How we act on CSAE

  • Removal: content that violates these standards is removed as soon as we become aware of it.
  • Termination: accounts involved in CSAE are permanently banned.
  • Reporting to authorities: we report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperate with law enforcement as required by applicable law, including preserving evidence where legally mandated.
  • Prevention: our Terms prohibit sexual content entirely, users can block any account, private profiles and message controls ("Who Can Message Me") let users limit contact, and our moderation team reviews all user reports.

Compliance with child safety laws

BlipVibe complies with applicable child safety laws in the regions where it operates, including CSAM reporting obligations under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A in the United States.
